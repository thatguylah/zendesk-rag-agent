title: How to reset your Northwind Cloud password
category: Account & Access
---

If you're locked out of your Northwind Cloud account, you can reset your password from the login screen.

1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?"
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. You'll receive a password reset link by email within 5 minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.
4. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If it expires, request a new one.
5. Choose a new password that is at least 12 characters and includes a number and a symbol.

If you don't receive the reset email after 15 minutes, your account may be using Single Sign-On (SSO). SSO accounts cannot reset passwords directly — contact your workspace admin instead.

Note: Password resets do not affect API tokens or existing active sessions on other devices.
