title: Understanding your invoice and billing cycle
category: Billing
---

Northwind Cloud bills monthly in advance, based on the number of active seats on the 1st of each billing cycle.

- Adding seats mid-cycle: you're charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in the current cycle, billed immediately.
- Removing seats mid-cycle: the seat remains active (and billed) until the end of the current cycle; no refunds are issued for partial months.
- Annual plans are billed upfront and receive a 15% discount versus monthly pricing.

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on file and are also available under Workspace Settings > Billing > Invoice History as a downloadable PDF.

If a payment fails, we retry automatically on days 1, 3, and 7 after the due date. After 3 failed attempts, the workspace is downgraded to read-only mode until payment succeeds — no data is deleted.

To change your billing contact or payment method, go to Workspace Settings > Billing > Payment Details. Only Workspace Admins and Billing Admins can make these changes.
