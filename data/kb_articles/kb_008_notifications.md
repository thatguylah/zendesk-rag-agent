title: Managing notification preferences
category: Product Usage
---

By default, Northwind Cloud sends email notifications for: task assignments, @mentions, due date reminders (24 hours before), and comments on tasks you're watching.

To change your notification preferences: click your avatar > Notification Settings. You can toggle each notification type independently for email, in-app, and mobile push.

"Digest mode" batches all non-urgent notifications (comments, watched-task updates) into a single email sent once daily at 9am in your workspace's local timezone, instead of sending them individually. Direct @mentions and assignments are never batched — they always send immediately regardless of digest mode.

To mute a specific project entirely, go to the project, click the bell icon in the top right, and select "Mute this project." Muting overrides your global notification settings for that project only.
